# Concert Ticket Checker

A concert has these rules:

- A ticket costs $45.
- A student ticket costs $30.
- VIP costs $90.
- A person can enter only if they have a valid ticket.
- Students must be 18 or younger to receive the student price.

Have the program calculate the total price for a person and 
create Boolean expressions answering questions such as:

Is the person eligible for the student price?
Is the ticket valid?
Can the person enter?
